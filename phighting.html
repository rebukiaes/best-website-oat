BLACKROCK

The KORBLOX Administration are the ruling faction of Blackrock. They are a military state who typically force their newspawn into science, engineering, or infantry fields. The KORBLOX Administration is ran by a shadowy group of higher-ups who have yet to be seen.

Biografts are robots mass produced by Blackrock, having been invented by Subspace with directives to accomplish specific programmed tasks. According to multiple Phighters, they are a relatively recent invention. There are multiple kinds of Biografts with specific skillsets and duties, and they are all based on variants of the Biograft Energy Swords gear.

LOST TEMPLE

Lost Temple is a hot, dry desert that was once home to a bustling city full of thousands of inhabitants. Ten years prior to the events of PHIGHTING!, this city was burnt to the ground in the Scorch, and the lasting effects thereof now serves to divide the remaining populace. Most inphernals that did not leave Lost Temple for other regions would end up joining either the Redcliff Wranglers or The Church of the TRUE EYE.

The temperatures of Lost Temple are unbearable to non-natives, and even those native to Lost Temple can find the environment unpleasant. Fashion in Lost Temple can be varied, but ponchos, scarves, bandanas, and cowboy hats are common fixtures of an inphernal's wardrobe. Those who live out in the desert plains tend to live in tents and sleep on hammocks.

PLAYGROUND

Playground is one of the four main regions in the Inpherno. It is an urban city as well as Windforce's domain.

Splintered Skies is a faction ruled by Windforce, one of the seven deities of the Inpherno. It occupies a floating island above Craterdust Capital, which was ripped out of the ground some years prior to the events of PHIGHTING!. Inphernals are able to be spawned directly into Splintered Skies. A large amount of inphernals live below Splintered Skies in Craterdust Capital. These inphernals tend to consider themselves factionless.

THIEVES' DEN

Thieves' Den is a lush, tropical region home to jungles, mountains, and various clans. Currently, the Darkage Clan is the only one known. It is known for its inphernal-eating flora and its vast foliage.

The Darkage Clan is the current ruling faction of Thieves' Den. Darkage Clan members are trained to be warriors as newspawn, and also accept capable warriors from other regions into their ranks, as seen with their recruitment of Katana. They have little tolerance for what diverges from their norm, as observed by their banishment of Vine Staff for her mutation, which they deemed as a "curse". They are also currently in an uneasy alliance with the Redcliff Wranglers, supporting them in their war against The Church of the TRUE EYE for control over the Lost Temple spawn.
