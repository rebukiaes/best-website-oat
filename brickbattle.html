Welcome to Brickbattle!

Active since 2006

Click on the tools to learn more about them!

Sword

The Sword is one of the most iconic Roblox tools. When clicked twice, you can perform a lunge, which has extremely high damage and another use. Despite seeming extremely useful for combat, it can easily be countered by rockets, so keep note of your opponents rocket cooldown when ambushing someone with a sword. The Sword grants a little bit of height when lunging in the air, granting you a small "double jump." Due to the Sword being able to instantly kill a player if you swing it around, it is one of the best tools for an ambush (especially from the air), or countering other feisty players.

Slingshot

The Slingshot has the ability to shoot gravity-affected pellets once activated. If a pellet bounces off of a part that isn't a player, its damage will be fully reduced. While the Slingshot's damage seems minor at first, add it up with the ability to hold it and no need to aim upwards (the Slingshot automatically shoots pellets to the mouse position if not too far), and you have a very decent close to mid range weapon.

Rocket

The Rocket Launcher is used mainly for close-combat and un-defendable destruction. Rockets are shot at a speed of 70 studs per second with gravity unaffected, and creates an explosion on impact. It can easily be countered by setting a wall infront of yourself using the trowel, but you can counter any opponent doing this by shooting a rocket upwards when a jump from them is suspected.

Trowel

The Trowel, when used, will summon a wall of bricks in the size of 4x1x3 studs. The wall is 3 bricks high and 4 bricks wide, but takes a while before it is fully built. Walls created from the trowel weld to the parts below them, attaching themselves to that part. Walls can be placed at any distance and on any surface or wall. Walls can also be used to block projectiles. This can be very useful against someone with their rocket still reloading, and even if the wall is destroyed, the trowel has a faster cooldown than the rocket does, allowing the player to constantly place trowels to block projectiles such as paintballs and usually superballs.

Bomb

The Bomb (aka Timebomb) can be effectively used to both destroy parts and eliminate other players. When the Bomb Tool is activated, a Bomb will be placed down and explode in approximately 3.8 seconds. The explosion created by the Bomb is very large and strong. You can also use it in combat using the Superball or Rocket to send it flying forward to kill enemies and destroy terrain or trowel walls. To counter other players knocking your bombs via projectiles, use a Trowel to block the opponents Superball from flinging it away.

Superball

The Superball is a long-ranged tool that shoots large balls that deal large amounts of damage to other players on contact. When impacted with another part, the superballs damage is halved (55 to 27.5 to 13.75 and so on) and it bounces at nearly the same speed as it was when impacting. Superballs are as fast as paintballs, but do not have semi anti-gravity, disabling the ability to travel as far as paintballs can, but the large damage and size can make up for it.

Paintball

The Paintball Gun is a long-ranged tool that shoots paintballs at high speeds that have semi anti-gravity and deal damage when they impact a player. It paints all parts that the projectile touches with a random BrickColor (unless a theme is equipped) other than some special parts. The distance a paintball can travel can exceed even 300 studs, which makes it the closest thing to a sniper.
