Top 5 of My Favorite Roblox Games

By Kenward

1. Burgundy vs Lapis

Click here to play

Burgundy VS Lapis ( BvL ) is a retro themed fast-paced symmetrical 10v10 FPS game, where your main objective is to eliminate the opposing team.

2. Forsaken

Click here to play

FORSAKEN is a Roblox asymmetrical survival horror game created and developed by the Forsaken Developer Cast. The game puts Survivors against a Killer in rounds with timers. Survivors must complete objectives and fight back to stay alive, while the Killer must hunt and eliminate all Survivors before the timer runs out. FORSAKEN is inspired by a non-Roblox horror game Dead By Daylight and a Roblox horror game The Robloxia: Until Dawn. FORSAKEN currently has three Chapters.

3. Tower Heroes

Click here to play

Tower Heroes is a Tower Defense game with a variety of unique Heroes to add to your arsenal. Battle against various enemies across a variety of different maps, each with their own unique bosses! Fight against other players in PVP mode, or team together to take down bosses in Freeplay mode and Campaign mode. Plenty of skins, stickers, modifiers, and more to collect.

4. The Battle Bricks

Click here to play

The Battle Bricks is a single-player tower defense styled game where you send units to defeat enemy bases to progress through stages! Although, the enemies will send units to attack back! Discover the seemingly countless amount of units along the way with varying purposes and upgrade them enough to see their alternative forms! Each stage and chapter will make you face new enemies, bases, bosses, or even how stages are handled to challenge your skills and strategies. A linear game that you can pick up from any time!

5. PHIGHTING!

Click here to play

PHIGHTING! is a class-based third person shooter reimagining classic ROBLOX gears and games with a modern twist! Team up with four other players to annihilate and conquer your enemies with 15 playable characters and over 25 maps to brawl on. Enjoy the original soundtrack, the unique character interactions, or just hang out with some friends in Crossroads, but always be ready to PHIGHT!
